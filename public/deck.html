The problem

A campus bins a year in a week, then buys it back.

04
Why it persists

Reuse fails on friction, not willingness.

Listing costs more than binning Eight fields versus twelve steps to the dumpster.
The alternative is a stranger Unverified, off campus, alone. Most students will not.
Drives run once a year Staffed and trucked. Nothing runs on a Tuesday.
05
The product

Four ways to keep an object in use. One tap that counts it.

Post in 20 secondsA photo and a sentence. No form to fight.
Your campus onlyOne verified university email. No strangers.
Meet on campusA library door, never a home.
Both confirm+1 handoff each. That tap is the measurement.

08
Logistics

No van, so no transport to subtract.

Collection round 2.5 kg CO₂e per object
Two students walking 0
Heavy things still moveA listing can ask for two people. Another student carries it, paid directly, and earns a public carry count.
Campus work, not a haulage billThe community is the logistics — which is why the transport term stays zero.

10 km round trip at the EPA's passenger-car factor, which flatters a van.

11
Money

Charged before a deal exists, never on the object.

Campus licenceAdmin tools, the move-out programme, an audited circularity number. The main line.
Verified placementsMovers and storage, one campus, one fortnight. Paid up front, never in ranking.
Free permanentlyPosting, claiming, giving, swapping. A fee on a give-away is a tax on generosity.
