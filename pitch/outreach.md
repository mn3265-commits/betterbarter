# Outreach — the traction gap, and how to close it in four days

The submission's weakest line is honest and it is ours: *"Not yet: paying
customers, an institutional partnership, or pilot users. Two verified Columbia
accounts."* Those two accounts are Agung and Tessa.

Judges discount shipped features — anyone can build. What they weight is
evidence that somebody outside the team wants this. **One named conversation
changes that line.** Not a partnership, not a pilot. A conversation, named.

Send these. Whether anyone replies before 2 September does not matter much;
having asked is itself the fact, and it is checkable.

---

## 1. EcoReps — send this first

**To:** ecorepscu@gmail.com
**Why them:** they champion Give + Go Green, run the Green Sale, and work with
Housing and Environmental Stewardship. They are the group already doing this
work, they are students, and students answer email. Highest chance of a reply
inside four days.

> **Subject:** A student-built reuse board for Columbia — would love 15 minutes
>
> Hi EcoReps,
>
> I'm Agung, an SPS student. With a classmate I've built BetterBarter, a reuse
> board for one campus at a time — students give away, sell or swap the things
> they'd otherwise leave behind, and each completed handoff is confirmed by both
> people so it can be counted rather than estimated.
>
> It's live at betterbarter.vercel.app and open source.
>
> I want to be clear about where it sits: it isn't an alternative to Give + Go
> Green. Those bins catch what's left at the end of a move-out. A board moves
> things between students before the pile forms, so the aim is to take the easy
> majority out of the pile and leave staffed collection to deal with what
> actually needs handling.
>
> Two things I'd genuinely like your view on:
>
> 1. Where does Give + Go Green take the most volume, and is any of it stuff
>    students would rather have handed to each other?
> 2. What would make you *not* want a board like this on campus?
>
> Fifteen minutes any time this week or next — or just a reply to those two
> questions, which is honestly more useful than a meeting.
>
> Agung Nugroho
> Columbia SPS · betterbarter.vercel.app

## What to write in the submission once any of this is sent

Replace the `[FILL]` in **"Have you achieved any traction?"** with whatever is
actually true on 2 September. Only ever the true version:

- *Nothing sent:* leave the bullet out. The honest "not yet" line already there
  is better than a vague claim, and judges can smell a vague claim.
- *Sent, no reply:* "We have written to Columbia EcoReps, who run Give + Go
  Green, and requested office hours with the AVP for the Office of
  Sustainability, to test where a peer-to-peer board sits alongside the
  collection programmes Columbia already runs."
- *Someone replied:* name them, name the date, and say in one sentence what they
  told you — including anything they pushed back on. A recorded objection reads
  as more real than a compliment.
